SaveSprout

Problem statement

Many students and young adults want to manage their personal finances effectively but find existing budgeting apps too complex and overwhelming. As a result, they often give up on tracking their income and expenses, experience financial stress, and struggle to develop healthy financial habits.

Technologies Used

  • Figma

Product goal

To create a simple, user-friendly budgeting app that helps students and young adults easily track income and expenses, gain clear insights into their spending, and build sustainable financial habits through supportive feedback and visual progress tracking.
