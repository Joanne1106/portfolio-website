Learn@RP

Problem statement

RP students find it difficult to learn new skills on the go because current platforms are not user-friendly and lack content tailored to their needs. As a result, learning becomes inconvenient and students lose motivation to upskill.

Technologies Used

  • Figma

Product goal

To design a new mobile app, Learn@RP, that aims to empower RP students to develop new skills through short, high-quality video lessons and structured minicourses. The app provides a personalised learning experience that helps students explore topics beyond their modules, build industry-relevant skills, and track their learning progress in a flexible and engaging way.
