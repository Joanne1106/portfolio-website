Shopping List App

Project Overview

This shopping list app helps users stay organized and shop efficiently by allowing them to add, edit, and delete items with ease. Users can categorize items into Groceries, Household, or Snacks, mark them as purchased, track prices, and even preview item images for quick recognition. With its simple and intuitive interface, the app is perfect for managing shopping lists, avoiding forgotten items, and keeping track of spending.

Technologies Used

  • React Native
  • CSS

Features

  • Add, edit and delete shopping items.
  • Categorize items into Groceries, Household or Snacks.
  • Mark items as purchased and track prices.
  • Preview item images for quick recognition.
  • Receive instant confirmation messages for actions.
